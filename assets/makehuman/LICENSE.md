# MakeHuman assets in this preview

The original MakeHuman system asset **shoes05** (including socks) and the MakeHuman Hair 01 asset **culturalibre_hair_02** (hair by culturalibre) are CC0 1.0 Universal. Their OBJ, MHMAT and MHCLO metadata record the CC0 release.

- Catalog: https://static.makehumancommunity.org/assets/assetpacks/makehuman_system_assets.html
- Download: https://files2.makehumancommunity.org/asset_packs/makehuman_system_assets/makehuman_system_assets_cc0.zip
- Source licensing: https://github.com/makehumancommunity/makehuman/blob/master/LICENSE.md
- CC0 legal text: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/legalcode
- Credited source holders: Data Collection AB, Joel Palmius, Jonas Hauquier / MakeHuman Community.

Adaptations: source meshes scaled and positioned on the existing head and foot bones; shoe pair split by side for attachment; hair shaded brown with its original diffuse/normal textures. Original shoe and sock textures retained. No purchases or paid services were used. An earlier sock-free derivative (`trainers.obj`) is not loaded; the user approved retaining socks.

This is an appearance evaluation. The original project and approved animation remain unchanged. The original character's source is documented separately in `../exercise-model-license.md`.
